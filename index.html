What we do

We test the future before it ships

Most AI and robotics demos die on contact with a real kitchen. We find the use cases that survive.

Home pilots

We place assistants and robots in everyday households for weeks at a time, and watch how families actually use them once the novelty wears off.

Field reports

Every pilot ends in a plain-language report — what people loved, what they abandoned, and why. No hype, just what happened.

Prototype lab

When a use case shows promise, our lab builds a rough version fast so partners can test demand before committing a product line to it.
