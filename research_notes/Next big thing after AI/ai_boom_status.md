## 1. Hyperscaler AI/data-center capex (2024, 2025, 2026 guidance; latest quarter)

Capex is still accelerating, not slowing. Combined 2026 guidance for Microsoft, Alphabet, Amazon and Meta is about $720–740B, roughly double 2025 (about $376–410B) and about 4x 2024 (about $186B). Its calendar-2026 figure was reportedly trimmed from about $190B to about $170B, attributed to lease accounting and delayed third-party GPU deliveries rather than weaker demand. Meta's Q3 spend also came in below consensus.

## 2. AI share of global VC funding (2025, 2026 YTD); mega-rounds

### Takeaway
AI's share of venture dollars peaked in Q1 2026 and has declined two quarters in a row. Crunchbase shows the global share falling from about 80% in Q1 to 64% in Q3; PitchBook shows US deal value falling from 88.4% to 65.9%. The drop came mainly because OpenAI and Anthropic did not raise in Q3, not because of a broad pull-back. Total Q3 venture funding (about $159B) and the count of $1B+ rounds (27, a record) remain extremely high, and AI's share is still 14 points above Q3 2025.

### Cited Findings
- **Crunchbase, Q3 2026 global:**
  - AI startups raised about $102B, 64% of about $159B in total venture funding. The AI share was down from about 80% in Q1 and more than 70% in Q2, but 14 points above Q3 2025 (implying about 50% in Q3 2025 **[OLDER DATA]**) — [TechJack Solutions (citing Crunchbase)](https://techjacksolutions.com/ai-brief/ai-startup-funding-102b-q3-2026-crunchbase/); [AI Bacon](https://aibacon.net/posts/crunchbase-q3-2026-venture-funding-159b-record-27-billion-dollar-rounds-ai-share-64-percent-100m-seed-rounds.html)

## 4. Enterprise adoption and ROI

### Takeaway
Adoption is rising steadily but remains a minority of US firms, at about 18–24% of employer businesses per the Census BTOS in 2026, and is concentrated in large firms and the information and finance sectors. McKinsey's 2026 survey shows scaling rising (44%, up from 38%), but enterprise-level EBIT impact is flat at 37% of respondents, and "high performers" are flat at about 6%. This is a "deployment without proven P&L payoff" picture, not a collapse.

### Cited Findings
- **US Census BTOS:**
  - US business AI use was 19.8% as of 3 May 2026. - **McKinsey State of AI 2026 ("On the road to ROI"):**

## 5. Signs of slowdown or bubble; is it a shift from hype to deployment?

### Takeaway
2026 has clear market stress:
- The Nasdaq entered a correction (more than 10% off its 6 Oct high) in November.
- Nvidia's stock fell about 24% from 29 Oct to 13 Nov.
- Oracle's credit is under pressure (CDS about 295 bps; ratings on negative watch).
- AI-linked debt issuance has surged, and the BIS and IMF have flagged circular financing.
- 30–50% of large data centers due in 2026 face delays, mainly from power constraints.

Yet fundamentals (Nvidia guidance, cloud growth, capex guides) kept rising through Q3 2026. Gartner places GenAI in the Trough of Disillusionment and agentic AI at the Peak. Taken together, the evidence fits a **financial-market and hype correction layered on still-accelerating physical spend**. "Shift from hype to deployment" is a fair description of the enterprise layer, but not of the infrastructure layer, which is still in expansion.

### Inferences
- **Bear evidence:**
  - Equity drawdowns (Nasdaq correction, Nvidia about −24%)
  - Credit stress concentrated at Oracle and in SPV and lease structures
  - Debt issuance roughly doubling YoY
  - Negative hyperscaler FCF
  - Regulator warnings (BIS, IMF)
  - Flat enterprise EBIT impact
  - Falling AI share of VC since Q1
  - Power-driven project delays
- **Bull evidence:**
  - Capex guidance raised or held for 2026 and guided up for 2027
  - Nvidia data center revenue +110% and a guide to $130B
  - Accelerating Azure and AWS growth
  - About $1.8T in cloud backlog
  - Lab run-rates growing several-fold YoY
  - A record count of $1B+ venture rounds
  - Anthropic's IPO book reported covered at a valuation of more than $1T
- **Net:** As of mid-Nov 2026, the premise "the AI boom is slowing down" is **not supported by spending or revenue data**, which are still accelerating. It **is partially supported by market prices, credit spreads and VC share**, which peaked in Q1 to Q4 2026 and are now correcting. The more accurate framing is a valuation and financing correction plus an enterprise "trough of disillusionment," while the physical buildout is constrained more by power and supply than by demand.
- **Watch items:** Anthropic IPO trading (19 Nov onward), Q4 capex prints (Jan–Feb 2027), whether 2027 capex guides actually exceed $1T, Oracle ratings actions, and the next BTOS readings.
