## 1. GLP-1 / obesity drugs: revenues, users, oral approvals, forecasts, prices

### Takeaway
The GLP-1 market is very large and still growing fast, but Lilly has clearly pulled ahead. Lilly's Q3 2026 revenue was $24.4B (+39% y/y), and it raised its FY2026 guidance to $91–92B. Novo's US injectable Wegovy is shrinking, and its new oral Wegovy pill (about $810M in Q3 2026) is what is holding up its numbers. US use jumped from 3% of adults (2024) to 11% (mid-2026). Prices are falling: realized US prices are down, cash prices on TrumpRx run $149–$350/month, and Medicare now covers these drugs at $245/month with a $50 copay.

### Inferences
- Lilly's tirzepatide franchise alone is running at about $64B a year (Q3 2026 at least $16B × 4). That already matches or beats most 2030 forecasts for the whole market made in 2023–24, so the older forecasts look too low.
- The market is splitting into two kinds of growth. Volume is rising fast (Lilly US volume +41%), while price is falling (−7% realized). TrumpRx and Medicare pricing push revenue per patient down while access widens.
- Survey data suggest US use roughly quadrupled from 2024 to 2026. That makes GLP-1s one of the fastest-adopted drug classes on record.

## 2. Gene editing: Casgevy, personalized CRISPR, in vivo pipelines, funding

### Takeaway
Gene editing is commercially real but still small. Vertex's Casgevy made $99M in Q3 2026, and more than 750 patients have started treatment. The bigger shift is in regulation. After baby KJ's bespoke base-editing therapy (early 2025), the FDA issued draft "plausible mechanism" guidance in Feb 2026 that creates a pathway for N-of-1 editing therapies, and an umbrella trial for urea-cycle disorders is planned.

### Cited Findings
- Casgevy revenue (ACTUAL):
  - Q1 2026: $43M. Q2 2026: $76M. Q3 2026: $99M (+30% q/q; Q3 2025 was $17M).
  - More than 750 patients have started treatment worldwide, with more than 100 starts in each of 4 straight quarters.
  - Infusions in the first 9 months of 2026 were twice the total for all of 2025.
  - FDA expanded the label in July 2026 to children aged 2 and up with SCD or TDT.
  - Vertex expects Casgevy plus Journavx to exceed $500M in 2026 (FORECAST). — [Vertex Q3 2026 release via CRISPR Medicine News](https://crisprmedicinenews.com/press-release-service/card/vertex-reports-third-quarter-2026-financial-results/); [Motley Fool transcript (3 Nov 2026)](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/11/03/vertex-vrtx-q3-2026-earnings-call-transcript/); [Vertex Q2 2026](https://investors.vrtx.com/news-releases/news-release-details/vertex-reports-second-quarter-2026-financial-results)
- Baby KJ: CHOP marked one year on 25 Feb 2026 since KJ, who has CPS1 deficiency, became the first person to receive a personalized CRISPR therapy. It was a base editor delivered to the liver by lipid nanoparticles and was designed in about 6 months. — [CHOP](https://www.chop.edu/news/childrens-hospital-philadelphia-marks-one-year-anniversary-worlds-first-personalized-crispr)
- FDA draft guidance (23 Feb 2026) sets up a "plausible mechanism" pathway for individualized therapies, both gene editing and ASOs, for ultra-rare diseases. Variant-specific versions of one editor can be treated as one product. The comment period reportedly closed on 27 Apr 2026. — [Clinical Trials Arena](https://www.clinicaltrialsarena.com/news/fda-unveils-new-pathway-to-usher-bespoke-therapies-to-market/)
- CHOP/Penn plan an umbrella trial for 7 urea-cycle disorders (7 genes) on the KJ base-editing platform. — [CHOP](https://www.chop.edu/news/childrens-hospital-philadelphia-marks-one-year-anniversary-worlds-first-personalized-crispr); [Friends of Cancer Research](https://friendsofcancerresearch.org/blog/from-innovation-to-implementation-what-baby-kj-reveals-about-the-future-of-personalized-therapies/)
- Beam Therapeutics has reportedly dosed 31 patients across its base-editing pipeline and plans an FDA filing by end of 2026. This comes from a secondary source and is unverified. — [Pulse.bot aggregator](https://www.pulse.bot/healthcare/news/personalized-crispr-therapies-could-soon-reach-thousands-heres-how-73c2e471-29e8-40b1-8644-62750ee2d4fa/)

### Inferences
- Casgevy's run-rate is about $400M a year by Q4 2026, still tiny next to GLP-1s. Gene editing's "next big thing" case therefore rests on in vivo and platform/N-of-1 economics rather than current revenue.
- The FDA's platform-style pathway could matter more for scaling than any single approval, because it removes the need for a separate trial for each variant.

### Gaps
- The status of Intellia's in vivo programs in 2026 (nex-z for ATTR, lonvo-z for HAE) was not retrieved. Intellia paused the nex-z MAGNITUDE trials in late 2025 after a liver-safety event (prior knowledge; not verified this session).
- Verve (acquired by Lilly in 2025) and other in vivo pipeline updates were not retrieved.
- No 2026 gene-editing-specific VC funding total was found.
- Whether the FDA plausible-mechanism guidance has been finalized was not confirmed.

## 3. AI drug discovery and biotech capital markets

### Takeaway
AI-discovered drugs have reached late-stage trials. Insilico's rentosertib (IPF) started Phase III in July 2026, with first patient reportedly dosed in October 2026. Isomorphic Labs raised $2.1B (May 2026) but, as of mid-2026, had not disclosed a clinical candidate. Biotech capital markets reopened in 2026. Biopharma VC reached $23.9B year-to-date through Q3 and IPO proceeds already exceed every full year from 2022 to 2025.

### Cited Findings
- Insilico rentosertib (TNIK inhibitor, IPF):
  - Phase IIa results were published in Nature Medicine (71 patients, 22 sites in China), with liver-enzyme safety signals noted.
  - A Phase III was registered on 7 Jul 2026 (NCT07687459): 320 patients, 47 centres in China, primary endpoint FVC decline.
  - The first Phase III patient was reportedly dosed in October 2026 (single source). — [Clinical Research News (8 Jul 2026)](https://www.clinicalresearchnewsonline.com/news/2026/07/08/insilico-medicine-launches-phase-iii-trial-for-ai-developed-drug); [IntuitionLabs](https://intuitionlabs.ai/articles/ai-discovered-drugs-clinical-trials-2026)
- Isomorphic Labs (Alphabet/DeepMind):
  - Raised a $2.1B Series B on 12 May 2026.
  - Had no disclosed clinical candidate as of mid-2026.
  - Hassabis guided to clinical trials by end-2026, slipped from end-2025 (FORECAST). — [IntuitionLabs](https://intuitionlabs.ai/articles/ai-discovered-drugs-clinical-trials-2026); [PharmaVoice H1 2026](https://www.pharmavoice.com/news/biotech-venture-capital-funding-2026-first-half/825084/)
- Biopharma VC:
  - 2026 year-to-date through 30 Sep: $23.9B across 385 rounds. Q3 2026 was $6.7B, down from $9.9B in Q2.
  - 2026 is on pace to beat 2025.
  - IPO proceeds and counts in 2026 already exceed each full year from 2022 to 2025. — [J.P. Morgan Biopharma Q3 2026 deck (DealForma data)](https://www.jpmorgan.com/content/dam/jpmorgan/documents/cb/insights/outlook/jpm-biopharma-deck-q3-2026.pdf)

- IPO counts conflict by method:
  - Pharma Source (PitchBook/Fierce data): 12 biotech IPOs raising $4.4B in Jan–Jun 2026.
  - BDO: only 5 within its own analysis window.
  - A PitchBook analyst called it a "bifurcated reopening". PitchBook forecasts crossover rounds of about $4.22B across about 28 rounds in 2026. — [Pharma Source](https://pharmasource.global/content/expert-insight/3-things-biotechs-need-before-going-public-in-2026/); [BioSpace](https://www.biospace.com/business/steady-stream-of-crossover-rounds-stock-biotechs-ipo-pipeline); [Fierce Biotech](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/biotech/when-markets-opened-we-were-ready-why-biotech-ipos-are-back-2026); [Foley (Nov 2026), "what an open window really looks like"](https://www.foley.com/es/insights/publications/2026/11/biotech-is-showing-what-an-open-window-really-looks-like/)

### Inferences
- The proof point for AI drug discovery has moved from "can AI find molecules" to "can they pass Phase III". Rentosertib's Phase III readout, likely 2028 or later, is the key test, and the trial is China-only.
- AI-bio money is concentrating in a few very large rounds (Isomorphic $2.1B). Headline VC totals therefore overstate how broad the recovery is.

### Gaps

### Inferences
- Starlink is the economic engine of the space "next big thing". It is an $11B+ revenue business that doubled subscribers in a year, and it funds Starship. Most of the space economy's value is still downstream services, not launch.
- The Space Foundation's 12% growth in 2025 is faster than the roughly 9% CAGR needed for $1.8T by 2035. Still, the two use different scopes, so the comparison is directional only.
- Defense (Golden Dome) gives the industry a large, policy-dependent demand floor. Much of it depends on reconciliation bills passing.
