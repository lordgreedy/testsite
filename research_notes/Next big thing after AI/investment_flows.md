Method note: research done 2026-11-18 using web search. Crunchbase News and PitchBook PDF hosts could not be fetched directly (blocked in this session's network), so most figures come from search-result summaries of reports from PitchBook, Dealroom, Space Capital, JPMorgan, the Fusion Industry Association and others, plus secondary aggregators. Treat aggregator-only figures (flagged below) as lower confidence. Trackers define sectors differently, so their totals are NOT directly comparable across sources.

Context baseline: Crunchbase reports global VC of $159B in Q3 2026 (up 53% YoY, down 25% QoQ), $679B YTD through Q3 2026 (the highest for that period on record), with AI taking $102B (64%) of Q3 — [Startupwired summary of Crunchbase, 2026-10-05](https://startupwired.com/2026/10/05/global-startup-funding-reaches-159-billion-in-q3-2026/); [aiweekly](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/crunchbase-logs-27-billion-dollar-rounds-in-q3-2026-as-ai-grabs-102b-or-64-of). So non-AI sectors share roughly 36% of venture dollars.

## VC funding by sector, 2024 vs 2025 vs 2026 YTD: which sectors are growing fastest?

### Takeaway
On money-flow evidence, the fastest-growing non-gen-AI sectors in 2026 are **defense tech** (global and especially Europe, where funding nearly tripled), **space** (record quarters driven by mega-rounds and the SpaceX IPO), **nuclear (fission and fusion)** and the **datacenter-power slice of climate tech**, and **quantum computing** (a record 2025, still accelerating in 2026). Humanoid/physical-AI robotics is growing fast but sits on the AI boundary. Biotech is recovering, not booming. Crypto VC was weak through 2025, and the stablecoin activity is small-ticket.

### Inferences
- Draft ranking by 2026 momentum (growth plus absolute level), using the best available evidence:
  1. **Defense tech**: largest absolute non-AI category ($35 to $41B in H1 2026 alone, depending on tracker), and Europe up about 3x. Highest-conviction signal.
  2. **Space**: $68B in H1 2026 (Space Capital, broad definition, inflated by SpaceX) against $17B in Q4 2025. Very high but lumpy.
  3. **Nuclear fission and fusion**: smaller dollars (fusion about $4.5B a year; nuclear VC $2.4B in 2025) but the steepest multi-year growth (+1,234% over 3 years) and a growing share of climate megadeals, pulled by datacenter power demand.
  4. **Quantum**: about $3.9B in 2025 (a record) and still "accelerating" in 2026; the capital mix is shifting toward growth and public markets.
  5. **Climate (datacenter-adjacent energy)**: large ($26B in H1 2026, up 55%) but driven by AI power demand, while carbon removal is collapsing.
  6. **Robotics/physical AI**: fast-growing but overlapping with AI; the data is poor.
  7. **Biotech**: a steady recovery ($23.9B biopharma VC YTD), not a breakout.
  8. **Crypto/stablecoins**: low VC dollars; the activity is in public markets and policy instead (see the IPO and government sections).
- Much of the "non-AI" momentum is AI-derivative: power for datacenters (nuclear, geothermal), physical AI (robotics), and AI-enabled autonomy (defense). A truly orthogonal "next big thing" is hard to see in the flows. Defense and space are the most independent of the AI cycle.
- Concentration is extreme in all the hot sectors (one Anduril round was about a third of Q2 defense funding; the top 10 quantum deals took about 60%), so totals are fragile to single deals.

## Government industrial-policy spending

### Takeaway
State capital is now the biggest force pointing toward defense autonomy, chips/critical minerals and nuclear. In the US, the FY2027 request seeks about $54B for drones and autonomy (part of a $1.5T total), and the government has taken direct equity stakes (about $27B across about 30 companies). The EU is lending €150B for defense through SAFE. China's 15th FYP (2026 to 2030) names quantum, embodied AI, biomanufacturing, hydrogen and fusion, BCI and 6G as "future industries". Japan has pledged $550B of US investment, partly into nuclear.

### Cited Findings
- **US defense FY2027 request**: about $1.5T total ($1.01T discretionary, up 44% from FY2026, plus $350B mandatory via reconciliation); about $53.6B for the "Drone Dominance" initiative/autonomous systems (including $39.2B in multi-year procurement and industrial-base investment), mostly via the Defense Autonomous Warfare Group; Golden Dome $17.5B to $17.9B — [Defense News, 2026-04-21](https://www.defensenews.com/news/pentagon-congress/2026/04/21/pentagon-seeks-funds-for-golden-dome-drones-ai-in-largest-ever-budget-request/); [MeriTalk](https://meritalk.com/articles/from-golden-dome-to-drones-breaking-down-dods-1-5t-budget-request/); [DoW Comptroller FY2027 request](https://comptroller.war.gov/Portals/45/Documents/defbudget/FY2027/FY2027_Budget_Request.pdf). Final appropriations status was not confirmed.
- **US equity stakes ("state capitalism")**: about $26.7B across about 30 equity or quasi-equity deals (count ranges from 30 to 41 by tracker, as of mid/late 2026); a 9.9% Intel stake from converted CHIPS grants (reportedly worth about $42B against $8.9B cost); a DoD $400M investment for about 15% of MP Materials plus a $110/kg NdPr price floor; 6 chipmakers added; quantum and rare-earth firms make up much of the rest — [Fortune, 2026-07-26](https://fortune.com/2026/07/26/the-u-s-government-invested-27-billion-in-corporate-stakes-good-luck-finding-them/); [CSIS](https://www.csis.org/analysis/understanding-federal-equity-investments-strategic-companies); [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/article/trump-administration-adds-6-chipmakers-to-the-governments-stock-portfolio-which-now-spans-30-companies-140535555.html)
- **Nuclear / Japan deal**: an $80B US government partnership with Westinghouse for new reactors, partly funded through Japan's $550B US investment commitment (to be deployed by 2029); the US gets 20% of Westinghouse profits above $17.5B (another source reports an 8% stake, conflicting) — [CSIS](https://www.csis.org/analysis/trade-deals-and-new-chapter-american-nuclear)
- **EU**: SAFE €150B defense loan instrument; the Commission approved the first 8 member-state plans (about €38B) on 2026-01-15; the Council approved 18 states between February and April 2026; about €11.9B was disbursed in 9 first payments by 2026-08-28 (third-party tracker; Poland €6.56B) — [European Commission](https://defence-industry-space.ec.europa.eu/commission-approves-first-wave-defence-funding-eight-member-states-under-safe-2026-01-15_en); [Consilium](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/policies/safe/); [Großwald tracker](https://www.grosswald.org/safe-defence-loans-tracker/)
- **China, 15th Five-Year Plan (2026 to 2030, approved by the NPC around 2026-03-12)**: names quantum technology, biomanufacturing, hydrogen and fusion energy, brain-computer interfaces, embodied intelligence and 6G as future industries — [gov.cn, 2025-10-25](https://english.www.gov.cn/news/202510/25/content_WS68fc10abc6d00ca5f9a0703e.html); [CGTN, 2026-03-05](https://news.cgtn.com/news/2026-03-05/China-to-foster-emerging-industries-and-future-technologies-1LgoSd9ePqE/p.html); [China Briefing](https://www.china-briefing.com/news/chinas-15th-five-year-plan-recommendations-key-takeaways-for-foreign-businesses/). A MIIT official says "6G will be a top priority" — [China Daily, 2026-08-27](https://mobile.chinadaily.com.cn/cn/html5/2026-08/27/content_013_6a8f417fed50be540e736b71.htm). PostQuantum claims $17.5B for three regional quantum venture funds (unverified) — [PostQuantum](https://postquantum.com/quantum-computing/china-15th-five-year-plan-quantum/)
- Legal and oversight risk: a Lawfare analysis argued that several Pentagon equity stakes may be unlawful, and Fortune notes they appear in no budget document — [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/07/26/the-u-s-government-invested-27-billion-in-corporate-stakes-good-luck-finding-them/)

### Inferences
- US/China/EU differences: the US is using direct equity, price floors and a huge autonomy procurement budget (demand-pull for drones, missile defense, chips, minerals, nuclear). The EU is using debt-financed defense procurement (SAFE), which is driving its defence startup boom (European defence VC up about 3x). China is targeting a broader "future industries" list, with quantum, embodied AI, BCI, 6G, hydrogen and fusion and biomanufacturing all named, and its priorities overlap the US/EU only on quantum, robotics and fusion.
- Overlap across all three blocs (quantum, robotics/embodied AI, fusion/nuclear, defense autonomy) is the strongest "money plus policy" signal.

## Thematic ETF and public-market flows

### Takeaway
